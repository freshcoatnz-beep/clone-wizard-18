## Best-practice tips

- **Ask the same day you finish** — customers are happiest immediately after the job is done.
- **Ask for specifics** — reviews that mention the suburb and type of work help you rank locally. Example: "Michael painted our Fendalton villa exterior."
- **Follow up once** — if they don't respond after 3–5 days, send one polite reminder.
- **Respond to every review** — reply within 24 hours and naturally mention the work: "Thanks for the kind words about your Cashmere exterior repaint!"
- **Never buy or fake reviews** — Google will remove them and can suspend your profile.

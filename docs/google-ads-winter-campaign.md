## Account structure

Two Search campaigns so we can control spend per service and read results cleanly.

| Campaign | Daily budget | Share |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Winter Interior – Christchurch | $11/day | ~70% |
| 2. Roof Painting – Christchurch | $5.50/day | ~30% |

Bidding: **Maximise Clicks** for the first 2 weeks (data gathering), then switch to **Maximise Conversions** once you have 15+ tracked conversions.

Network: Search only. **Turn OFF Search Partners and Display Network.**

## Conversion tracking (do this BEFORE launching)

1. **Phone calls from ads** — Tools → Conversions → New → "Phone calls from call assets". Count after 60 sec.
2. **Quote form submissions** — Google Ads conversion tag fired on the form Thanks state. I can wire this in once you have the conversion ID + label.
3. **GA4 linked to Google Ads** with "Generate lead" imported as a conversion.

Without this you're flying blind — set it up first.

## What to expect

- **Week 1:** 5–10 clicks/day, learning phase. Change nothing.
- **Week 2:** Pause keywords with 30+ clicks and zero conversions. Mine Search Terms for new negatives.
- **Week 3–4:** First 2–5 leads. Target CPL: $40–80 per quote request.
- **Month 2:** Switch to Maximise Conversions once 15+ conversions tracked.

If CPL > $120 after 30 days, rework the landing page before increasing budget.
